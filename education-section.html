ZipTide & the Golden Zipper — a story game for young learners

A free, bilingual (English / Turkish), voice-narrated browser game that teaches children aged 5–6 the central idea behind our project: the gut has a protective barrier that must stay just closed enough — and when it loosens, it can be gently repaired. No reading required: every scene is read aloud.

Ages 5–6 Bilingual EN / TR Voice-narrated Runs offline Open source
Open the game full screen →

Why we made it

Explaining a computationally designed peptide and intestinal barrier biology to a preschooler is hard — so we turned our science into a fairy tale. Children meet the tiny guards who hold a golden zipper closed, watch a curious visitor loosen it, and then help our hero close it again, tooth by tooth. Playing it, a five-year-old rehearses the exact logic of our project without a single technical word.

Learning objectives

After playing, a child can:

  • recognise that the body has a protective barrier that keeps it safe;
  • explain that the barrier should let good things in and keep harmful things out;
  • understand that the barrier can be loosened, and that it can be repaired;
  • take away a gentle value: mending is stronger than breaking.

The science behind the story

Every character maps to a real part of our project, so the game is a first step on a ladder that older students and parents can climb via the note below.

In the storyIn real biology / our project
The little guards holding handsIntestinal epithelial cells joined by tight junctions
The golden zipperThe gut barrier — the tight-junction seal that holds the wall together
Letting in water drops & crumbsSelective permeability: nutrients pass, harmful things are kept out
Curious Zonulin opening the zipperZonulin, a real protein that loosens tight junctions and increases permeability (a “leaky” barrier)
The hero ZipTideZipTide, the peptide we designed in silico to help restore barrier integrity
Closing the zipper, tooth by toothRe-establishing the barrier so the tissue is protected again

Designed for young children & for reuse

  • Pre-reader friendly: every scene is narrated aloud by one of our students, so children who cannot yet read can play independently.
  • Bilingual: a one-tap language switch (English / Turkish) with matching text and audio, widening the audience.
  • Simple & safe: one clear tap-or-drag action per scene, large touch targets, no text input, no accounts, and no data collection.
  • Accessible & robust: works on phones, tablets and computers, runs fully offline, and falls back to device text-to-speech if a clip cannot load.
  • Open & reusable: a single self-contained file released open-source, so any educator or iGEM team can host it, embed it, or run it from a USB stick with no setup.
